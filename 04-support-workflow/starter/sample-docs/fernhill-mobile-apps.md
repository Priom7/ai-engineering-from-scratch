# Fernhill Cloud Mobile Apps

Fernhill Cloud is a fictional file-sync service for small teams, used as sample data for this project. This page describes the iPhone and Android apps.

## Supported devices

The mobile app runs on iOS 16 or later and Android 11 or later. Tablets are supported on both.

## Offline files

Mark a file or folder as available offline to keep a copy on the device. Offline files update automatically the next time the device is online.

## Camera upload

Camera upload copies new photos and videos to a Camera Uploads folder. By default it only runs on Wi-Fi. You can allow mobile data in Settings.

## Passcode lock

You can protect the app with a 6-digit passcode, Face ID or fingerprint. After 10 wrong passcode attempts, the app signs out and removes offline files from the device.

## Notifications

The app can notify you when someone shares a file with you or comments on one of your files.
