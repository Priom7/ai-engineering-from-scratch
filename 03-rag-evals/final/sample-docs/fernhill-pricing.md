# Fernhill Cloud Pricing

Fernhill Cloud is a fictional file-sync service for small teams, used as sample data for this project. This page lists the plans and what they cost.

## Plans

Starter costs £6 per user per month and includes 100 GB of storage per team. Team costs £12 per user per month and includes 1 TB per team. Business costs £20 per user per month and includes 5 TB per team.

## Annual billing

Paying yearly costs the same as ten months, so you get two months free. Annual plans are billed upfront for every seat.

## Seats

Starter supports up to 10 users. Team and Business have no user limit. Seats added in the middle of a billing period are charged pro rata on the next invoice.

## Add-on storage

Extra storage is sold in packs of 500 GB for £8 per month, on any plan.

## Nonprofit pricing

Registered charities get 30% off the Team and Business plans. Apply from the Billing page with your charity registration number.

## Free trial

Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial. No payment card is needed to start the trial.
