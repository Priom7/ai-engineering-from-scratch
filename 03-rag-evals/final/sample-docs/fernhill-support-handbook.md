# Fernhill Cloud Support Handbook

Fernhill Cloud is a fictional file-sync service for small teams, used as sample data for this project. This handbook describes how the support team works.

## Support hours

Live chat is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 18:00 UK time. Email support at support@fernhill.example is answered seven days a week. Business plan customers get a one-hour response target. All other plans get a response within one business day.

## Severity levels

Severity 1 means the service is down for everyone on the account. Severity 2 means a core feature is broken but there is a workaround. Severity 3 covers questions, how-to requests and minor bugs.

## Escalation

If a Severity 1 ticket has no response within 30 minutes, the on-call engineer is paged automatically. Business plan customers can also call the emergency phone line, which is not available on other plans.

## Data export and deletion

An admin can export all account data from Settings, then Data, then Export. Exports are ready within 24 hours. Deletion requests are completed within 30 days and cannot be undone.

## Passwords and two-factor authentication

Support staff will never ask for a password. If a member loses their two-factor device, an admin can reset it from the Members page. If the only admin is locked out, support verifies their identity with the last invoice number and the last four digits of the payment card.

## Storage limits

The Starter plan includes 100 GB per team, Team includes 1 TB, and Business includes 5 TB. When a team goes over its limit, new uploads pause, but existing files stay available.
