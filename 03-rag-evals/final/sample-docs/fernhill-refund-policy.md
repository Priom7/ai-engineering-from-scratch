# Fernhill Cloud Refund Policy

Fernhill Cloud is a fictional file-sync service for small teams, used as sample data for this project. This policy explains when and how customers can get their money back.

## Monthly plans

You can cancel a monthly plan at any time from Settings, then Billing. Cancelling stops the next renewal. We do not refund the current month, but you keep full access until the end of the billing period.

## Annual plans

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, we refund the unused months minus a 15% early-cancellation fee. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

## How to request a refund

Email billing@fernhill.example with your account email and invoice number, or use the Request refund button on the Billing page. Every request is reviewed by a person within 2 business days.

## What is not refundable

Add-on storage packs, domain registrations, and purchases made with promotional credit are not refundable. Accounts closed for breaking the Acceptable Use Policy are not eligible for refunds.

## Downgrades

If you move from the Team plan to the Starter plan in the middle of an annual term, the difference is added to your account as credit. It is not refunded to your card. Account credit never expires while the account is open.

## Outages and service credits

If monthly uptime falls below 99.5%, you can claim a service credit worth 10% of that month's fee. If uptime falls below 99%, the credit is 25%. Claims must be made within 14 days of the end of the affected month.
